## Evidence rules

- **Directly documented:** a release identifies an agreement, published specification, launch, or stated target. Cite the specific release and preserve its date, variant and units.
- **Author inference:** conclusions about scale, portfolio differentiation and process governance follow from the documented facts but are **analytical proposals**, not confidential company results.
- **Unresolved:** 180 hp factory-baseline story, engine-specific financial returns, actual lifetime production by family, controlled comparative reliability and a causal link between Ghosn's legal case and this engine. None is asserted as fact.

## Scope note on Ghosn

The author's initial research question included a story about a piano case, an escape and a book. The book is documented by its publisher [S11]. The escape is outside the mechanism being analyzed here, and the exact container and helpers are unnecessary to the powertrain decision. Nissan's original investor statement [S10] states its own allegations; the engine-agreement theory is unsupported. This editorial choice keeps a compelling anecdote from becoming a false causal claim.
